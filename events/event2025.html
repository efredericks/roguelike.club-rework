Roguelike Celebration 2025

When and Where?

Roguelike Celebration 2025 was held on Saturday and Sunday October 25 - October 26, 2025 as a virtual event in our custom-built multiplayer game/chat space. Talks were also streamed to Twitch and YouTube. We will have individual videos up soon. In the meanwhile, there are full recordings available for the first 3 blocks of Saturday, the rest of Saturday, and Sunday.

There was also be a free preview event September 7, 2025, 3pm Pacific in the same social space, with talks streamed to Twitch and YouTube. We will be uploading these as individual videos soon, but in the meanwhile you can watch the full recording here.
