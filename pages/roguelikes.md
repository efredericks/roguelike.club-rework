# What are roguelikes?

Roguelike games ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roguelike)) are intricate and fun computer games that have a history back to 1980 — and they've influenced many games along the way (including Diablo, Spelunky, and Dwarf Fortress). The most famous roguelike is probably [Nethack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roguelike), which looks like this:

![Nethack](https://www.roguelike.club/screenshot.png)
